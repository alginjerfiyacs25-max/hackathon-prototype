# Three-to-five minute demo

**0:00–0:40 — Context.** Introduce AquaSentinel as a simulation dashboard for hilly-region flood coordination. Point out the prototype disclaimer.

**0:40–1:30 — Baseline.** Explain overall risk, village ranking, shelters, and the map. Click a village to show its risk and prototype warning window.

**1:30–2:20 — Trigger.** Select Flash Flood Risk and run the simulation. Highlight changed scores, red markers, shorter impact windows, and alert recommendations.

**2:20–3:20 — Decide.** Open the highest-priority village. Show the recommended shelter and route warnings. Explain that NetworkX excludes blocked roads and that allocation respects capacity.

**3:20–4:00 — Trust.** Show factor contribution and synthetic model metrics. Emphasize explainable logic, human decision-making, and that no alert is actually transmitted.
